# Questionnaire

Ask after the session named in brackets. Read each question as written. Do not explain, lead, or fix the answer. Write what is said, in the child's words. Do not state or hint at any time target. If the child does not answer, write `no answer`.

## Tester A, child wording
Scale where asked: 1 = not at all, 5 = a lot (show five circles; pointing is fine).

1. [after any game] Was it fun? Why? (1-5, and what you said)
2. [A, B] How easy was it to make the player do what you wanted? (1-5) Which part was hardest? Which part was easiest?
3. [A] The glowing power chips at the bottom: what do you think they do? (their words only)
4. [A, B] Did a power chip feel fair? Did it feel like a cheat, or just a power? (their words)
5. [B, C] Did anything big happen in the game that you remember? What? How did you know it was big?
6. [A, B, G, H] Could you read the words and see the buttons? Was anything too small, too bright, or hard to see?
7. [B, C] Did you see short camera moments in the game? How did you feel about them? Too many, too few, or just right? Did you want to skip any?
8. [B, C] Was the game too long, too short, or just right?
9. [A, K] Do you want to play again now? Why or why not? What would you do next?
10. [A] Was there anything you had to guess how to do?
11. [anytime] Is there anything you would change?
